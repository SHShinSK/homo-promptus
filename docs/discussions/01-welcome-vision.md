### EN

Welcome to **Homo Promptus** — an open, gentle satire project about how we use AI and agents.

We observe that humans may increasingly **only ask or only delegate**. This repo is not here to shame anyone. We mock **habits and UX extremes**: one-line orders, unread diffs, collapsed sources.

**Two species (modes) in one portal**

| Species | Stance |
|---------|--------|
| **Homo Promptus** | Leaves intent, context, and verification in prompts — “serious today-speak” |
| **Homo Delegans** | Delegates even thinking and checking — light parody |

Switch mode in the app and feel how copy, defaults, and density change. Ask yourself: *which species was I today?*

**Live demo:** https://shshinsk.github.io/homo-promptus/  
**Contribute:** small PRs welcome — Museum cards (`content/cards/*.json`), copy (`src/lib/i18n.ts`), i18n, UI mode splits.

**Ground rules (short)**

- No real-name mockery of people or companies  
- Never frame Delegans as “stupid” — only convenience, speed, “the bot will know”  
- Code: MIT · Content/cards: CC0  

**Questions for this thread**

1. What resonated with you — Promptus, Delegans, or hybrid?  
2. What should we build next (see [ROADMAP](https://github.com/SHShinSK/homo-promptus/blob/main/ROADMAP.md))?  
3. Who else should we invite (communities, languages, subspecies ideas)?

Reply in **English, Korean, or both**. Maintainers will fold good ideas into the roadmap.
